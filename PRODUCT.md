# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential clients and art directors evaluating Arthur Shakov's work on an initial portfolio visit.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual portfolio that presents selected digital-design projects and gives prospective collaborators a concise sense of the author's practice.

## Positioning

A personal, terminal-inspired showcase where project curation and the browsing experience express the author's craft.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically scan the portfolio, filter work, inspect project previews, and may follow links to live projects.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static bilingual site built with Node.js, SCSS, and vanilla JavaScript (with Lenis & GSAP).
- The rendered page remains usable without JavaScript.
- Planned atmospheric audio uses two supplied tracks with a smooth crossfade and requires an explicit visitor action to begin playback.

## Brand Commitments

- Terminal-inspired interface and restrained, minimal presentation.
- Bilingual English and Russian experience.
- Music is an optional atmospheric layer, never an interruption.

## Evidence on Hand

- Curated project metadata and screenshots in `src/data/` and `screenshots/without-url-bar/`.
- Audio tracks: `audio/Filtered_Aperture.mp3` and `audio/Through_the_Glass.mp3`.

## Product Principles

- Let selected work remain the main focus.
- Make the interface feel authored, precise, and calm.
- Preserve visitor control over enhancement features.
